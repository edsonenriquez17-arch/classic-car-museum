The legacy

For decades, Jimmy has rescued, restored, and preserved automotive history in the heart of Lima.

From the World's Oldest Car to rare Porsche classics, each vehicle has an engine, a story, and a soul.

This is not a garage. This is a living Museum. And your experience starts here.

A Collection That Makes History

ARTECH brings together 96 exceptional vehicles, the result of decades of Jimmy's passion and dedication to preserving automotive history. Each piece stands out for its design, engineering, and historical significance, making the collection a unique legacy of elegance, innovation, and automotive culture in Lima, Peru.
